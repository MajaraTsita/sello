About Lesotho Travel

Welcome to Lesotho Travel your gateway to the Kingdom in the Sky. We are dedicated to showcasing the breathtaking beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality of Lesotho.

Our wool blankets adorned with symbolic patterns represent Basotho identity and pride.

From traditional music and dance to handcrafted woolcraft and the symbolic Mokorotlo hat worn widely across the country, culture here is lived daily — passed down through storytelling and communal gatherings.

Mission

To showcase Lesotho's unique landscapes and culture while promoting sustainable tourism that empowers local communities.

Culture

Experience vibrant Basotho traditions, from woolcraft and folk music to the iconic Mokorotlo hat and storytelling.

Why Visit

Discover dramatic mountain trails, pony trekking, waterfalls, and the serene beauty of an untouched alpine nation.
